RHYTHMIC LIBERATION

PRIDE

COMMUNITY • HEARTBEAT • POWER

Mila Tina transforms the stage into a site of collective liberation, celebrating the pulse of the community through high-octane rhythmic performances.

Her work centers the queer body in motion, honoring feminine energy and the power of rhythmic embodiment. From main stages to immersive wellness activations, Mila creates rituals of connection that resonate long after the final beat.
