CORE THEMES

Feminine cognition / Rhythm as structural language / Emotion as data / The body as interface / Perception and constructed narrative / Memory as collective archive

BIO

Mila Tina is a Chilean-American interdisciplinary artist whose work operates at the intersection of performance, technology, and somatic inquiry. Through drumming, movement, biometric data, and visual design, she constructs sonic-kinetic systems where the body functions as both medium and message. Her performances explore rhythm as memory, tension as structure, and emotional complexity as compositional intelligence, disrupting binary narratives around gender, control, and perception.
