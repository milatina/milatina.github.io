MILA TINA
NEW YORK / CHILE

Interdisciplinary artist, performer, and experience designer blending rhythm, biometric technology, visual storytelling, and movement.

CORE THEMES

Feminine cognition / Rhythm as structural language / Emotion as data / The body as interface / Perception and constructed narrative / Memory as collective archive

BIO

Mila Tina is a Chilean-American interdisciplinary artist whose work operates at the intersection of performance, technology, and somatic inquiry. Through drumming, movement, biometric data, and visual design, she constructs sonic-kinetic systems where the body functions as both medium and message.
