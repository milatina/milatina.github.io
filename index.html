CORE THEMES

BIO

Mila Tina is a Chilean-American interdisciplinary artist whose work operates at the intersection of performance, technology, and somatic inquiry. Through drumming, movement, biometric data, and visual design, she constructs sonic-kinetic systems where the body functions as both medium and message. Her performances explore rhythm as memory, tension as structure, and emotional complexity as compositional intelligence, disrupting binary narratives around gender, control, and perception.

HERRATIC

TYPE

Multidisciplinary Live Performance

OVERVIEW

A multidisciplinary solo work integrating live percussion, movement, martial precision, and time-based media. The piece interrogates the cultural framing of femininity as volatile or excessive and rearticulates emotional complexity as structured intelligence.

11

BIOSYMPHONY

TYPE

Bio-Data Performance / Responsive Installation

OVERVIEW

Real-time biometric data including heart rate and brainwaves drive a live soundscape and visual environment. In solo or duet format, the piece reveals coherence and dissonance through rhythm and relational tension.
